# Laboratory – E471 Power System Analysis I

This folder summarizes laboratory studies conducted for **E471 – Power System Analysis I** during the 2025–2026 Fall term.  
All experiments were performed using **PSS®E** and focused on practical analysis of large-scale power systems.

> Detailed lab reports, raw data, and screenshots are not publicly shared to comply with academic integrity policies.  
> This README provides a high-level overview of objectives, methods, and key concepts.

---

## Laboratory 1 – Voltage Collapse Analysis
- Investigation of voltage collapse phenomenon under increasing active and reactive power demand  
- Identification of voltage collapse (blow-up) points for selected buses  
- Construction and interpretation of **P–V curves**  
- Analysis of the impact of line impedance on voltage stability  
- Discussion of mitigation methods without changing load demand  

**Key concepts:** Voltage stability, P–V analysis, reactive power support

---

## Laboratory 2 – Voltage Regulation
- Identification of buses with voltage magnitude violations  
- Evaluation of system voltage limits (0.95–1.05 pu)  
- Application of voltage regulation methods:
  - Shunt capacitors and reactors  
  - Generator voltage set-point adjustments  
- Analysis of the impact of swing bus voltage control  

**Key concepts:** Voltage regulation, reactive power compensation, generator control

---

## Laboratory 3 – Power Flow Control
- Detection of overloaded transmission lines and transformers  
- Application of power flow control methods:
  - Transformer tap ratio adjustment  
  - Phase-shifting transformers  
  - Reactive power compensation using shunt devices (FACTS modeling)  
- Assessment of system performance before and after control actions  

**Key concepts:** Power flow control, transformer phase shifting, FACTS devices

---

## Laboratory 4 – Contingency Analysis
- Evaluation of system security under normal operating conditions  
- Execution of **N–1 contingency analysis**  
- Identification of:
  - Line and transformer overloads  
  - Bus under-voltage and over-voltage conditions  
  - Network islanding scenarios  
- Proposal of corrective actions to restore secure operation  

**Key concepts:** System security, contingency ranking, N–1 analysis
